About

A working meeting for neurotech builders

The Computational Neuroscience Workshop (CNW) brings together students and professionals from engineering and medicine to explore how the brain computes and how we can build technology around it. Over two full online days, participants will move from the fundamentals of neural modelling and signal analysis to machine learning for brain data and the hardware that makes wearable neurotechnology possible. Clinicians will see what engineering can offer, and engineers will see the clinical problems worth solving. Following the success of the first edition in December 2025, CNW 2026 is built around one idea:The best neurotechnology is built by people who understand both sides.
