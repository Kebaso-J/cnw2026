Prof. Pierpaolo Sorrentino, MD, PhD

Institut de Neurosciences des Systèmes (Inserm and Aix-Marseille University, France)

Talk

Building the Virtual Brain: Patient-Specific Models for Clinical Medicine

Prof. Pierpaolo Sorrentino, MD, PhD, is a Principal Investigator at the Institut de Neurosciences des Systèmes (Inserm and Aix-Marseille University, France) and a tenured associate Professor at Parthenope University, Naples, Italy. He is a board-certified neurologist, and he holds a Ph.D. in Telecommunication Engineering. He subsequently worked as a postdoctoral researcher within the Human Brain Project, where he developed computational approaches to investigate large-scale brain dynamics, with a focus on Neurodegenerative diseases. His research has made extensive use of magnetoencephalography (MEG) to characterize functional brain networks and identify electrophysiological biomarkers, with a particular focus on critical dynamics. More recently, his group has focused on mechanistic whole-brain modeling, integrating electrophysiology, neuroimaging, and network science to infer latent physiological mechanisms underlying neurological diseases. His work was central to personalized computational models of Parkinson’s disease, multiple sclerosis, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, and other brain disorders, with the long-term goal of developing clinically actionable digital twins for diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment optimization. He has authored nearly 200 peer-reviewed scientific publications.

Confirmed

Anthony Gitau MSc

PhD Fellow, University of South-Eastern Norway

Talk

Trustworthy and robust AI in clinical settings: why models that look great on benchmarks struggle in real hospitals, and what to do about it.

Antony M. Gitau is a PhD Fellow at the University of South-Eastern Norway (in collaboration with Vestfold Hospital Trust), where his research focuses on AI method development for hematological image analysis, deep learning foundation models, and agentic AI systems for computational pathology. Previously, Antony earned an MSc in Mathematical Sciences (Artificial Intelligence for Science) with a distinction in his dissertation from the University of Cape Town and AIMS South Africa, supported by a Google DeepMind scholarship. His academic background also includes a BSc in Biomedical Engineering from Kenyatta University, Kenya. Antony’s professional experience spans international institutions and cutting-edge labs. He completed a research stint at Google Research (Nairobi), contributing to causal forecasting models for food insecurity prediction, and has served as a Data Science Africa (DSA) Research Fellow focusing on computer vision applications in orthopedic recovery. An active contributor to the broader AI and engineering community, Antony is a member of NOBIM and NORA-Tech, and has held leadership roles within IEEE Region 8 and KamiLimu.
